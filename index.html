02 — Selected work

What I built so far.

Three finished projects I created. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

The Appointment

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 3 weeks

A stealth based horror game set in an abandoned hospital centered around exploring an umfamiliar environment and trying to escape whilst avoiding a monster that is out to kill you.

My role: Everything - Player Mechanics, Enemy Mechanics, Environment and level design, Key and door unlock system, Audio and Ambience, Menus and HUD.

The Betrayal of Santa

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 3 weeks

A 2D Side Scrolling Platforner that is Christmas themed and is about how the North Pole has betrayed Santa and he must escape the North Pole on Christmas Eve night to make it in time to deliver the presents.

My role: Everything - Player Mechanics and Movement, Enemy Mechanics and movement, Sprites and Assets, Level Design and Environnment - Excluding the background, it is a borrowed free use asset - Menus and HUD.

Kaidan

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Made with a Partner · 9 weeks

A Fixed camera Survival Action Horror game with a PSX artstyle set in 1970's Japan where you wake up in a nightmare to explore a familiar home and fend off the horrors that lay within.

My role: Balanced role - Asset Creation (not all done by me), Player Mechanics, Enemy Mechanics, Combat and Projectile Mechanics, Music and Audio creation, Fixed Camera Perspective Mechanics, Key and Door unlock system, Door Opening Cutscene, Menus and HUD.

03 — About

About me

I am a Second-Year Level 3 Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I joined the course because I've always loved and played games my entire life and felt as though it would be even more interesting to learn how to make games and pursue a career in that.

I am most interested in gameplay programming — the systems that decide how a game feels and plays - This year I have worked in C++ and Blueprints in Unreal Engine 5, modelled and textured my own assets in Blender and Substance Painter, and recorded and implemented my own audio and music I have written for my games using Audacity.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project to develop my skills further.

Tools

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • C++ and Blueprints
  • Blender
  • Substance Painter
  • Reaper and Audacity
  • Git and GitHub

Strengths
